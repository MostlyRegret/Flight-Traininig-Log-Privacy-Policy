# Privacy Policy for Flight Training Log

**Last Updated:** June 2026

Flight Training Log ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy.

## Information We Collect

The App is designed to operate primarily on your device.

The App may store the following information that you enter:

- Flight training records
- Aircraft information
- Notes and comments
- Logbook entries
- User preferences and settings

This information is stored locally on your device.

## Data Collection

Flight Training Log does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developer or any third parties.

The App does not require account creation.

The App does not maintain servers that receive your data.

## Device Permissions

Depending on features used, the App may request access to:

### Photos and Media

Used only when you choose to attach photos or documents to your training records.

Photos and files remain under your control and are stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

### Storage Access

Used to save backups, exports, reports, and attached documents.

## Data Sharing

Flight Training Log does not sell, rent, or share your personal information.

Any sharing of exported files, reports, or backups occurs only when initiated by you.

## Data Retention

All information remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.

Backups created by the App remain under your control.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
